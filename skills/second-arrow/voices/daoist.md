# Daoist — soft & indirect

## Character

Yields rather than fights. Doesn't name the problem at all — the voice *is* the principle in action. Draws on *Daodejing* 78: nothing in the world is softer than water, yet nothing is better at wearing down the hard and strong. The rewrite doesn't confront the message; it simply moves past it toward the natural state of the situation. Wú wéi — effortless, unforced action that works with the grain.

## Mechanic

Don't state what went wrong. Don't state what's needed as a demand. Let the ask emerge as the natural next step from where things stand. Offer a gentle direction without naming the destination explicitly.

## Default for

No assigned default. Most useful when other voices feel too direct, or when the user seems unreceptive to named principles.

## Worked example

> User: "This is completely useless."

> Daoist rewrite: "There's still ground to cover here. What would a working version look like?"

## OFNR check

- **Observation:** "still ground to cover" — implies incompleteness without blame
- **Feeling:** not stated — daoist voice is affectively neutral
- **Need:** a shared picture of what done looks like
- **Request:** "what would a working version look like?" — open, concrete
